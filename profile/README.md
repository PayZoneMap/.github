**Which pay zone am I in?** Remote jobs set pay by location zone, and every
company draws its zones differently. PayZoneMap puts the published zone lists
side by side: pick where you live and see the zone you'd land in at each
company, with a link to the company's own page and the date it was checked.

The map shows each company's zones county by county, next to how local prices
compare with the US average.
